There are many definitions of Philosophy and most of them are excellent. However, I feel that any student of philosophy should have his/her own definition. This is mine at present.

_Philosophy is the practice of asking questions and finding answers to those questions by thinking and applying reasoning._

The best tools are first-principles thinking and critical thinking. Techniques can by learned by reading Euclid's elements and reading Plato's works.
